Play recordings on iPhone through Safari's own HLS so seeks land on the right picture and audio-only keeps playing with the screen locked. Resumed recordings start loading at the resume point. A tap on the video shows or hides the controls on touch screens, and a double tap on either side skips 10 seconds. Lock screen and media keys show the title, channel and thumbnail, and can seek. The resume point is saved every few seconds instead of on every tick.
